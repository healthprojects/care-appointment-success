Customer need. Health boards need to cut missed appointments and waiting times affordably, with Welsh-language parity, fair treatment and little staff burden. For patients the service is free. Health boards pay a predictable price per 1,000 appointments.

Product and business model:

- A modular subscription service with three parts: a bilingual communication and barrier-check platform; an optional analytics and fairness module; and a navigator service delivered through licensed voluntary-sector partners.
- Reusable non-software assets (the barrier taxonomy, content library and evaluation framework) made available across NHS Wales, to build trust and speed up adoption.

Timescales:

| Period | Activity |
|---|---|
| November 2026 to March 2027 | Phase 1 feasibility |
| [2027/28] | Phase 2 pilot and evaluation in live clinics |
| [2028] | Adoption by the health board and a first additional Welsh health board |
| [2029 onward] | Wider UK market |

On timing, a live pilot of the bilingual messaging and barrier check could start within about 3 months of a Phase 2 start, because Phase 1 delivers a tested prototype and an agreed integration route.

Delivery plan: Phase 1 evidence, then a Phase 2 pilot, then procurement through NHS Wales routes, such as health board contracts and all-Wales frameworks. Each health board is configured rather than custom-built.

Marketing strategy: evidence-led.

- Publish the Phase 1 and Phase 2 results.
- Present through NHS Wales planned-care networks, Life Sciences Hub Wales, health board innovation leads and Rural Health and Care Wales.
- Offer reference visits with the health board.

Scaling rapidly:

- Configuration rather than custom build.
- Cloud hosting.
- Content libraries shared across customers.
- A partner model for navigators.

What makes it unique:

- Barrier-aware, not reminder-only.
- Published, support-only fairness governance.
- Welsh parity in dynamic and voice content.
- A human navigator route.
- Closing the loop into slot backfill.
