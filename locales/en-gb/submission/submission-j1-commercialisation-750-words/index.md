Customer need. Health boards need to cut missed appointments and waiting times affordably, with Welsh parity, fair treatment and little extra staff work. For patients, the service is free. For health boards, the software is free and open source, and they pay only for service around it, at a predictable price per 1,000 appointments.

Product and business model (open source, paid service):

- The reference software is free under open licences, so there is no lock-in and any health board can inspect it.
- We charge for what a health board does not want to do itself: hosting in the UK, configuration for each board, Welsh and English content upkeep, support, assurance documents (clinical safety, data protection), and training.
- A navigator service delivered through licensed voluntary-sector partners.
- Reusable assets (barrier taxonomy, content library, evaluation framework) are free across NHS Wales, to build trust and speed adoption.

Scaling: configuration and not custom build, cloud hosting, shared content libraries, a partner model for navigators, and open code that others can run.

Competitive advantage:

- It asks why, and links the answer to help, instead of only reminding.

- A human route through navigators.
- It closes the loop from cancellation to refilled slot.
- It is open and cheaper to adopt than closed platforms. Suppliers can add reminders, but are slower to add all of this together, especially Welsh parity.
