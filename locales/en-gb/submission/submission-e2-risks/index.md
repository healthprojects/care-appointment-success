Risks are rated Red (high), Amber (medium) or Green (low), before and after action, under the required headings. T is technical, C commercial, E environmental. We review the register weekly and report it to the steering group monthly.

Safety statement. Phase 1 carries no direct patient-safety risk, because no live messages are sent and no real patient data is used in the prototype. The design aims to improve safety: urgent appointments get a person's follow-up, a patient who cannot attend is rebooked and not lost, and no score can lower anyone's care.
