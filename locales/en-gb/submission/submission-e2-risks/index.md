Risks are rated Red (high), Amber (medium) or Green (low), before and after mitigation, under the required headings. Each is marked technical (T), commercial (C) or environmental (E). We review the register weekly and report it to the steering group monthly.

Safety statement. Phase 1 carries no direct patient-safety risk because no live messages are sent. The design is meant to improve safety: urgent appointments get human follow-up, and patients who cannot attend are rebooked rather than lost from the pathway.
