How the service reduces environmental impact:

- A missed appointment can mean a wasted journey and an unused clinic session. Releasing slots early lets the time be reused and avoids repeat journeys.
- A phone or video appointment, where a clinician has approved it for that type of clinic, can avoid some journeys.
- Letters have the highest footprint, so the service moves to digital where the patient chooses it, and keeps paper for those who need it.
- The reference software is free and open, so health boards do not pay in cost or carbon to rebuild the same thing.
