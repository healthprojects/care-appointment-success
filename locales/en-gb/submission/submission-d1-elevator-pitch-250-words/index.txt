About 64,000 outpatient appointments in Cwm Taf Morgannwg go unattended every year. Behind each one is a person who did not get care, and a slot nobody else could use.

Most people don't skip on purpose. A letter arrived late or was hard to read. The bus doesn't run. A child needs collecting. A shift can't be swapped. The phone number on file changed. Language barriers cause misunderstandings.

Care Appointment Success does four things for every appointment:

1. Asks. A clear Welsh or English reminder, by text, voice call or letter, answered in one tap or word: yes, no, or a different time.
2. Helps. If someone can't come, a short "what's in the way?" question points to help that already exists: travel cost refunds, carer support, a different time, or a phone or video appointment where a clinician agrees. Anyone who needs a person gets a call from a local community navigator.
3. Hands back. Slots released early are offered to people waiting for care.
4. Learns. We publish what worked, split by language and by area, so gaps narrow.

A fair, explainable tool helps staff decide who to phone first. It can only add support. It never overbooks, penalises or reduces anyone's care.

What is new is the combination: one bilingual service, built on a free open source prototype, ideally heading towrad NHS Wales systems and tested for fairness.

Result: fewer missed appointments, shorter waits, fairer access, healthier patients.
