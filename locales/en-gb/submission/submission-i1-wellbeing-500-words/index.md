Care Appointment Success improves long-term wellbeing in Wales by helping people get planned care, with the largest gains aimed at those who find it hardest to attend.

The seven well-being goals of the Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015:

| Goal | Example from this project | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| A healthier Wales | More people get timely planned care | DNA rate (modelled in Phase 1, measured in Phase 2) |
| A more equal Wales | Support aimed at deprived, disabled, caring and digitally excluded people | Gap in missed appointments between the most and least deprived areas: reported, and never widened |
| A Wales of vibrant culture and thriving Welsh language | Welsh speakers get an equal experience | All content in both languages; at least 10 of 30 usability testers are Welsh speakers; parity is a pass criterion |
| A Wales of cohesive communities | Local navigators link people to local support | Community organisations in the signposting directory |
| A prosperous Wales | Less time lost from work and caring through missed or repeated visits | Patient-reported time saved |
| A globally responsible Wales | Fewer wasted journeys and clinic sessions | Estimated journeys and carbon dioxide avoided |
| A resilient Wales | Free reusable assets cut duplicated effort across health boards | Assets published for NHS Wales |

The five ways of working:

- Long term: the barrier taxonomy, content library, evaluation framework and open-source reference software can be reused by every health board.
- Prevention: barriers are found and removed before an appointment is missed, and early release helps prevent longer waits.
- Integration: the service links health, council and voluntary-sector support, and works with NHS Wales systems rather than replacing them.
- Collaboration: it is delivered with Welsh partners, the health board and community organisations.
- Involvement: patients, carers, Welsh speakers, disabled people and booking staff co-design and test it.

Fairness is built in. The support tool can only trigger more support. It never reduces appointments or contact, and its fairness audit by deprivation, age, sex and language is published.

These are targets that Phase 1 tests. Phase 1 sends no live patient messages, so benefits at population level are measured in Phase 2.
