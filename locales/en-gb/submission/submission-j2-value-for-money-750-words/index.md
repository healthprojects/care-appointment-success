Value for money for Wales:

- Phase 1 pays back if about 400 missed appointments are avoided. At about £150 of lost value per missed appointment, that is about £60,000, and about 0.6% of one year's roughly 64,000 missed appointments.
- Each one-percentage-point fall in the 8.6% missed-appointment rate would free about 7,400 appointments, worth about £1.1 million of capacity a year for Cwm Taf Morgannwg alone. These are estimates, not cashable savings.
- The service reuses existing NHS Wales infrastructure instead of replacing it.
- Modular adoption means health boards buy only what they need.
- Data is portable and interfaces are open, so there is no lock-in.

Financial viability after the project:

- Recurring revenue comes from per-appointment subscriptions. The navigator service is funded through licensed partners.
- Phase 1 builds the affordability model. It will give a running cost per 1,000 appointments, a benefit-to-cost ratio with a sensitivity range, and a break-even number of health board customers.
