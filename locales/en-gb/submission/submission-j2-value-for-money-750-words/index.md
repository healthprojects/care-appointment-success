Value for money for Wales:

- Phase 1 repays itself if about 400 missed appointments are avoided. At about £150 of lost value each, that is about £60,000, and about 0.6% of one year's roughly 64,000 missed appointments.
- Each percentage point off the 8.6% DNA rate frees about 7,400 appointments, worth about £1.1 million of capacity a year in Cwm Taf Morgannwg alone. These are estimates, not cashable savings.
- The software is free and open source, so a health board pays for service and support and not for a licence. Nothing is paid twice if another board adopts it.
- It reuses NHS Wales infrastructure instead of replacing it, and boards can adopt one module or all.
- Data is portable and interfaces are open, so there is no lock-in.

Financial viability after the project:

- Income comes from fees for hosting, configuration, content upkeep, support and assurance, charged per 1,000 appointments. Navigator services are funded through licensed partners.
- Phase 1 builds the affordability model: running cost per 1,000 appointments, a benefit-to-cost ratio with a sensitivity range, and a break-even number of health board customers. Our target is a ratio of at least 3:1. [Add an illustrative cost per 1,000 appointments and break-even once the cost model is drafted.]
