How the costs relate to the project goals:

- Staff time (68 days, £34,000) delivers the core work: discovery and baseline analysis, the bilingual prototype, the risk model and fairness audit, and the integration assessment.
- The Welsh-language partner (£4,000) and accessible formats (£2,500) deliver the Welsh and English parity and accessible formats the challenge requires.

Value for money for the taxpayer:

- At about £150 of lost value per missed appointment, avoiding roughly 400 missed appointments would pay back the whole Phase 1 cost. That is about 0.6% of one year's roughly 64,000 missed appointments.
- Phase 1 is small and low-risk. It uses fictional appointments and existing infrastructure, and it ends in two go/no-go gates, so the funder commits to Phase 2 only on evidence.
- About 69% of the cost is delivery staff time and about 20% is subcontractors, mostly fixed-price, so cost is predictable.
- The request is a modest share of the £200,000 portfolio, leaving room for complementary projects.
- Payment is only on completed milestones, with 20% held until the closure report. We ask for nothing in advance.
- The outputs, including the affordability model, are designed to scale across Wales, so the learning benefits other health boards.

Value for money for us: Phase 1 gives us the evidence to decide on Phase 2 and on adoption across Wales, at a cost we can bear without taking profit.
