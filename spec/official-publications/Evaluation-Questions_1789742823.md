## Evaluation Process

**Note:** All scoring is out of 5, with 5 being the highest and 0 the lowest.

## Assessment for Understanding

‘How well does the proposal meet the challenge?’ Does the supplier demonstrate an understanding of the challenge Brief and detail the full benefits?

## Assessment for Technical Validity

‘How valid is the technical approach?’ Has the supplier sufficiently described the technical deliverables and is the R&D approach suitably robust?

## Assessment for Innovation

‘How innovative is this project? How much does the project develop or employ novel concepts, approaches, methodologies, tools or technologies for this area?’

## Assessment for Care

If the proposal delivers what it says it is going to deliver, will it enhance care e.g. safety, compassion etc.

## Assessment for Impact

How positively does the solution impact the outlined problem and benefit the end user(s)?

## Assessment for Project Delivery

‘Is there a clear management plan? Are the milestones and evaluation procedures appropriate?’

## Assessment for Safety

Does the proposal provide you with enough assurance that there is no detriment to the safety of the user or that it improves it (e.g 5=improves safety 0=detrimental to safety)?

## Assessment for Risk

Have all appropriate risks been identified and has the supplier provided you with enough information that they will be adequately mitigated?

## Assessment for Project Team

‘Does the applicant have the skills, capabilities and experience to deliver the intended benefits?’

## Assessment for Readiness

‘Does the application provide sufficient confidence that the project can be delivered within the required timescales?’

## Assessment for Testing

‘Does the application provide sufficient confidence that the testing/evaluation approach outlined within the proposal is robust? Does it provide adequate assurance?’

## Assessment for Accessibility & Welsh Language Compliance

‘Has accessibility and Welsh Language been considered, and is the response to the question appropriate for the intended use of the solution and in line with Welsh Language standards? I.e., if answer no, suitable justification has been provided.’

## Assessment for Finance

Do you feel that the requested funding in the application provides good value for money against the work that they are going to carry out? Are the budget and costs realistic, justified and appropriate for the aims and methods?’

## Assessment for Social Value

‘How will the applicant deliver measurable economic, social, environmental and cultural benefits for Wales, beyond the core requirements of the contract?

## Assessment for Commercialisation

Are you confident that the business has detailed how they are going to get their product onto the market after this Project, including finance, funding streams and additional partners?

- is there a clear commercial potential for a marketable product, process or service
- is there a clear plan to deliver that and a clear marketing strategy
- how significant is the competitive advantage of this technology over existing technologies that meet the market’s needs

## Assessment for Timing

If project is successful and we wanted to purchase, would the solution be available in a timely manner to suit the needs of the challenge?

## Financial Viability

Does the solution look like it could be financially viable following completion of the Project, including any preferential terms?
