## APPLICATION PROCESS

All applications will be treated in confidence.

The application process for this Contracts for Innovation Cymru competition requires the completion of the online Application Form on the Simply Do Innovation Portal. Whilst a downloadable copy is available on the portal, all applications must be submitted digitally.

Before submitting, it is the lead applicant’s responsibility to make sure:

- that all the information provided in the application is correct
- your proposal meets the eligibility and scope criteria
- all sections of the application are marked as complete

The form should be completed and submitted as directed. We strongly advise that these accompanying Guidance Notes are read prior to submission.

You must submit the application before the competition deadline.

The application process closes at - see challenge brief

Please do not send completed applications by post or by any other means than as directed.

If you experience any difficulties or wish to seek additional clarification, please contact [ContractsforInnovationCymru@wales.nhs.uk](mailto:ContractsforInnovationCymru@wales.nhs.uk)

If you experience any difficulties with the sell2wales website please contact the helpdesk on 08445610672

## GUIDANCE ON COMPLETING THE APPLICATION FORM

These notes should be read in conjunction with the application form and are designed to help you to provide the information required. The application form has a fixed length for the answer to each question; if you enter text that is not visible within the cell, or submit appendices that exceed the set maximum length, it will not be read or assessed.

The application form should be completed using a font size no smaller than 10 (Arial). Keep the use of acronyms to a minimum. Only use acronyms where a term is mentioned frequently throughout the proposal. If you do choose to use an acronym, do not assume that the reader knows what it means, and be sure to define it, bearing in mind that individual sections of the application may be read separately during the assessment process.

In order for your application to be accepted you must submit all the required information. This includes all mandatory fields from the application form and failure to complete these fields will result in your application being rejected on the grounds that it is incomplete.

Guidance on the individual sections of the application form is provided below:

## D) YOUR SOLUTION

### QUESTION 1. PUBLIC FACING ELEVATOR PITCH

Please provide a brief ‘Dragon’s Den’ style public facing elevator pitch of your project.

*Word limit: 250 words*

Please provide a brief, "public facing” description of the project. Be clear about what makes it innovative and how it relates to the scope of the competition. How does it tackle different aspects of the challenge and how will it provide an integrated solution?

Should your project be successful, this information will be made public once the award is confirmed. Do not include any commercially confidential information in this section. We reserve the right to amend the description before publication, if necessary, but will consult you about any changes.

### QUESTION 2. VIDEO ELEVATOR PITCH

Please record and submit a video pitch (via the Simply Do platform) summarising the following:

- What your solution is and what makes it innovative
- The benefits it will deliver
- Why your project should be funded.

Maximum of 3 minutes in total

### QUESTION 3. PROPOSED TECHNOLOGY OR PROCESS

How does the project meet the challenge described in the competition scope?

*Word limit: 1000 words*

Describe:

- your proposed technology or process.
- current state of development.
- The proposed benefits.

Avoiding the use of unnecessary technical jargon, describe clearly how it will deliver the outcomes as described in the competition documentation. In particular describe the benefits that will accrue to UK householders and businesses. What evidence have you acquired to support your claims?

This question will be scored against this assessment criterion:

- How well does the proposal meet the challenge?
- Does the supplier demonstrate an understanding of the challenge Brief and detail the full benefits?

### QUESTION 4. TECHNICAL PROJECT SUMMARY

What are the main technical challenges you are addressing?

*Word limit: 750 words*

Explain:

- how you will address the challenge
- what the innovation is
- the main technical deliverables
- the research and development that will prove the scientific, environmental and commercial merit of the project
- what might be achieved by deploying the innovation to address the selected challenge

This question will be scored against this assessment criterion:

- How valid is the technical approach?
- If the proposal delivers what it says it is going to deliver, will it enhance care e.g. safety, compassion etc.

- How positively does the solution impact the outlined problem and benefit the end user(s)?
- Has the supplier sufficiently described the technical deliverables and is the R&D approach suitably robust?

### QUESTION 5. CURRENT STATE OF THE ART AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

What is the current level of innovation and what novel concepts will this project deliver?

*Word limit: 750 words*

Are similar products currently available in the market?

How is your proposed project differentiated from them?

You must include details of:

- any existing intellectual property (IP)
- its significance to your freedom to operate
- novel concepts you develop or employ
- new approaches or technologies you use
- new tools or technologies

This question will be scored against these assessment criteria: ‘How innovative is this project? How much does the project develop or employ novel concepts, approaches, methodologies, tools or technologies for this area?’.

## E) READINESS AND PROJECT PLAN

### QUESTION 1. PROJECT PLAN AND METHODOLOGY

Describe your project plan and identify the main milestones.

*Word limit: 750 words*

The plan must be comprehensive. The emphasis throughout should be on practicality.

You must:

- describe resources that will be needed to deliver the project
- describe what the main success criteria will be
- identify the project management processes that will ensure you achieve the milestones
- provide a clear plan with identified milestones
- explain how you would handle any intellectual property (IP) issues which might arise during the project.
- include details of how you will maintain freedom to operate and fulfil the IP requirements detailed in the contract if you are working with subcontractors.

Your milestones must:

- be clear
- be defined using SMART (specific, measurable, achievable, realistic and time-bound) criteria
- be associated with the appropriate deliverables and payments
- indicate your payment schedule by month
- Include clear stage-gates / “Go/No go” points

This question will be assessed against these assessment criteria:

- Are the timeframes realistic and will their proposal complete by the deadline date?
- Is there a clear management plan?
- Are the milestones and evaluation procedures appropriate?

Please note information from the finances section will be used to support the assessment of this question. Proposed milestones and associated payments stated in this section must match those entered in the finance summary on your application.

### QUESTION 2. RISKS AND MITIGATION

What are the key technical, commercial and environmental risks you foresee in the delivery of this project?

*Word limit: 750 words*

This question will be assessed against these assessment criteria:

- Have all appropriate risks been identified and has the supplier provided you with sufficient information to provide confidence that they will be adequately mitigated?

## G) ACCESSIBILITY & WELSH LANGUAGE COMPLIANCE

Creating accessible opportunities aligns with the principles of the [Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015](https://www.gov.wales/well-being-of-future-generations-wales). The act emphasises the importance of long term thinking and aims to improve the social, economic, environmental, and cultural wellbeing of Wales. In addition to this, Wales is a bilingual nation and Welsh language considerations must form an integral part of project design and delivery. Applicants should consider Welsh language, accessibility and equitable access together throughout the project, recognising the diverse needs of service users, communities and professionals across Wales.

Please indicate (using Yes, No or Not Applicable), will your organisation ensure that the proposed solution is accessible, inclusive, and available to users in both Welsh and English throughout the project (e.g. design and testing) and beyond, where applicable? If No, or not applicable, please explain why.

If responding yes, please describe how your organisation will demonstrate the following:

- Any planned accessibility improvements during the project period.
- How Welsh-speaking users will be able to access and use the solution.

This question will be scored against this assessment criterion: ‘Has accessibility and Welsh Language been considered, and is the response to the question appropriate for the intended use of the solution and in line with Welsh Language standards? I.e., if answer no, suitable justification has been provided.’

If external expertise is required for translation, these costs should be built into the overall project costs in Section H.

## SUPPORTING INFORMATION: BACKGROUND AND FURTHER INFORMATION

### ABOUT CONTRACTS FOR INNOVATION COMPETITIONS

More information about the Contracts for Innovation programme can be found on the Innovate UK website: [Contracts for Innovation - Innovate UK Business Connect](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/programme/contracts-for-innovation/)

Contracts for Innovation competitions are open to all eligible organisations that can demonstrate a route to market for their solution. Under current regulations, contracts are open to applications from organisations registered in the UK, European Union (EU) and the European Economic Area (EEA).

Contracts for Innovation is the procurement of R&D services. If successful, you will receive a contract to deliver the proposed activity. Costs quoted must reflect actual costs at a ‘fair market value’ and not include profit.

If you are VAT registered, your total costs are expected to include VAT that you would charge as a service provider. VAT is the responsibility of the invoicing business, and applications are expected to list total costs inclusive of VAT.

Suppliers for each project will be selected by an open competition process and retain the intellectual property generated from the project, with certain rights of use retained by the contracting authority. This is an excellent opportunity to establish an early customer for a new technology and to fund its development.

### NEXT STEPS

We will carry out checks to make sure you are an established company with access to the funds necessary to complete the project.

Please respond promptly to any requests for additional information to avoid any delays. We will also review your milestones, which, if not suitable, will need to be amended during project setup.

### YOUR CONTRACT

A standard Contracts for Innovation contract will be issued which you will need to sign and return before you start your project, and this must be completed within 30 days of being notified your application was successful.

Your contract will show the start date for your project, do not start your project before this date. Any costs incurred before your start date cannot be claimed as part of your contract.

### IF YOUR APPLICATION IS UNSUCCESSFUL

If you are unsuccessful with your application this time, you will be notified via email.

Feedback can be requested.

Sometimes your application will have scored well, and you will receive positive comments from the assessors. You may be unsuccessful as your average score was not above the funding threshold or your project has not been selected under the portfolio approach which is being applied to this challenge.
