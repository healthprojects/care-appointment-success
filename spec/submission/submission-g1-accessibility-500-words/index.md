Accessibility & Welsh Language Compliance

Creating accessible opportunities aligns with the principles of the Well-being
of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015. The act emphasises the importance of
long term thinking and aims to improve the social, economic, environmental, and
cultural wellbeing of Wales. In addition to this, Wales is a bilingual nation
and Welsh language considerations must form an integral part of project design
and delivery. Applicants should consider Welsh language, accessibility and
equitable access together throughout the project, recognising the diverse needs
of service users, communities and professionals across Wales.

Explain how your organisation ensure that the proposed solution is accessible,
inclusive, and available to users in both Welsh and English throughout the
project (e.g. design and testing) and beyond, where applicable?

Please describe the following:

- Any planned accessibility improvements during the project period.

- How Welsh-speaking users will be able to access and use the solution.
