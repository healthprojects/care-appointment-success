# Submission E2: risks 750 words

File `locales/en-gb/submission/submission-e2-risks-750-words/index.md`

What are the key technical, commercial and environmental risks you foresee in the delivery of this project? *

Guidance:

- List risks and issues that you anticipate with this project and any activities
  you can undertake to reduce their impact on the successful outcome of your
  project.

- Think about them with the following headings: Project Management, Material,
  Resources, Staffing, Patient Safety and Other.

- Score the risks in a Red, Amber, Green format (High, Medium and Low).
