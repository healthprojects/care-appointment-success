Please note if you are successful, you will need to provide a commercialisation plan/strategy as part of your contractual deliverables.

Describe how you plan to commercialise your product, expanding on how your project is unique within the market place, also to include any partners you will be working with, and any additional funding streams *

What is the commercial potential of your project?

You must focus on your proposed customer’s needs.

Describe your:

- timescales
- projects commercial potential for a marketable product, process or service, with a key focus on affordability to end user
- delivery plan
- your current marketing strategy
- potential to rapidly scale up your technology to cover wider markets
- competitive advantage that your project has over existing or alternative technologies that meet market needs
- any existing commercial relationships relevant to the project
- future commercial potential across the public or private sector and international markets with the focus on your proposed customer’s needs
