Provide a summary and justification of your project costs (including VAT)

Describe:

- How much will the project cost?

- How does it represent value for money for the team and the taxpayer?

- the total costs inclusive of VAT (If applicable) you are requesting in terms of the project goals

- how this project represents value for money for you and the taxpayer

All costs quoted must reflect actual costs at a ‘fair market value’ and not include profit.

The assessors are required to judge the application finances in terms of value
for money. They will score your finances against this assessment criterion: ‘Do
you feel that the requested funding in the application provides good value for
money against the work that they are going to carry out? Are the budget and
costs realistic, justified and appropriate for the aims and methods?
