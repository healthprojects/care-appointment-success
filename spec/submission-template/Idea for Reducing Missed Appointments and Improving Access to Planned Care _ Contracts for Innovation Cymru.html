G) Accessibility & Welsh Language Compliance

Supporting information

Creating accessible opportunities aligns with the principles of the Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015. The act emphasises the importance of long term thinking and aims to improve the social, economic, environmental, and cultural wellbeing of Wales. In addition to this, Wales is a bilingual nation and Welsh language considerations must form an integral part of project design and delivery. Applicants should consider Welsh language, accessibility and equitable access together throughout the project, recognising the diverse needs of service users, communities and professionals across Wales.

1. Will your organisation ensure that the proposed solution is accessible, inclusive, and available to users in both Welsh and English throughout the project (e.g. design and testing) and beyond, where applicable?
